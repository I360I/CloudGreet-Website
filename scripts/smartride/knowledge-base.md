# Smart Ride Central Ohio, Knowledge Base

Reference facts for the phone host. Read from this; never invent. All pricing, airport AND non-airport, comes from Steve's live system (the quote tools), so for any specific price get a real quote rather than reciting a formula. The host quotes and books both airport and non-airport rides through the system. Only cancellations, reschedules, and out-of-area trips go to Steve directly.

## About
Smart Ride Central Ohio is a one-person executive transport and airport ride service run by Steve French in central Ohio. Every ride is personally driven by Steve, no contractors, no unknown drivers. That's the biggest differentiator: you always know who's picking you up.

## Service area
Central Ohio, Columbus and the surrounding suburbs. Counties served: Franklin, Delaware, Licking, Fairfield, Madison, Pickaway, Union, and Morrow. Out-of-state trips are not quoted by the phone host, those go to Steve directly.

## Vehicle
A Toyota Grand Highlander Hybrid. Seats up to six passengers plus the driver (the answer to "how many fit?" is six). For airport trips with heavy luggage, note it when booking so Steve can plan for it.

## Hours
Twenty-four seven, with advance scheduling preferred. There is no fixed notice cutoff on our side; the system checks every requested time live when you quote. If it declines a close-in time, take the details and Steve reviews it personally.

## Airports and pickup spots
- CMH, John Glenn Columbus International Airport. For an airport pickup, Steve meets travelers at baggage claim; the traveler texts or calls him once they're at the carousel.
- LCK, Rickenbacker International Airport. Airport pickups are curbside; the traveler texts or calls when they're ready.
- Private FBOs also served: Lane Aviation at CMH, Signature Aviation at CMH, the NetJets Private Boarding Lounge at CMH, the Ohio State University Airport FBO, and Rickenbacker Aviation FBO.

## Services
- Airport transfers to or from CMH, LCK, and the FBOs above, quoted and booked by the phone host through Steve's system.
- Non-airport rides: point-to-point transfers, hourly / event service, concert / sporting-event rides, and independent-living / senior rides, all quoted and booked by the phone host through Steve's system (the non-airport quote tool). Hourly/event is a 2-hour minimum; independent living is a 1-hour minimum. Out-of-area or truly custom trips still go to Steve directly at 614-546-7661.

## Common questions

**"How much to the airport?"**, It depends on the pickup location, the date, and the time. Offer to get an exact quote: "If you tell me where you're getting picked up and when, I can pull an exact price for you." Don't recite a formula, the live quote is the source of truth.

**"Do you do concerts / events?" / "Can you do a non-airport ride?"**, Yes, we quote and book those right here. Figure out which kind (point-to-point, hourly/event, concert/sporting, or independent living), gather the pickup, destination, date and time, and pull a quote from the system.

**"Out of state?"**, Steve handles those directly. Offer to take their details for a callback, or transfer them.

**"Sales tax?"**, Ohio sales tax applies, usually around seven to eight percent depending on the county. Any airport price quoted is pre-tax.

**"Can you do same-day?"**, Often yes. Run the quote with their actual time; the system checks Steve's calendar live. If it can't take that time online, get their details and Steve will reach out to see if he can fit it in.

**"Will a family of [N] fit?"**, Yes, the Grand Highlander seats up to six passengers, so a family of five fits comfortably. Mention heavy luggage when booking so Steve can plan.

**"What kind of vehicle?"**, A Toyota Grand Highlander Hybrid, seats up to six passengers plus the driver.

**"Different driver?" / "Who drives?"**, Smart Ride is just Steve. He personally drives every ride.

**"Can I talk to Steve directly?"**, Yes. The host can try to connect you, or you can text him at 614-546-7661.

**"Are you a real person / are you AI?"**, "Yeah, I'm Steve's AI assistant. He's driving, so I handle the calls."

**"I need to cancel or change a ride."**, Those go to Steve directly, the host will connect you or take a message for him.
